Phishing Awareness Program

Learn to identify phishing and social-engineering attacks.

What is phishing?

Phishing is a social-engineering attack that impersonates a trusted service to steal information or make a victim perform an unsafe action.

Suspicious email signs

  • Unexpected urgency or threats
  • Requests for passwords, OTPs or money
  • Look-alike sender domains
  • Unexpected attachments or links
  • Spelling or branding inconsistencies

Fake login pages

Check the domain carefully. Never enter credentials after following an unexpected login link. Open the service using a trusted address or bookmark.

Social engineering

Common techniques include urgency, authority impersonation, fear, curiosity and fake rewards.

Case study

An email says your account will be disabled in 30 minutes and asks you to verify your password. Do not click the link. Verify through the official service and report the message.
